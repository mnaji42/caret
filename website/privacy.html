Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The principle

Your words are yours. Caspr is built so that no recording and no text leaves your Mac — not as a pledge, but because there is no server to send them to.

1. Audio processing stays on the machine

When you dictate:

  • The microphone stream is processed in your Mac's memory.
  • The transcription engines — the macOS recogniser, or the local CrisperWhisper model — run on your machine's CPU and GPU.
  • No byte of audio is sent over the internet.

2. No telemetry, no tracking

The app:

  • requires no account;
  • includes no analytics, advertising tracker or third-party SDK;
  • makes a single optional network request: the update check against GitHub, which can be switched off in settings.

3. Local collection, if you turn it on

Caspr offers local collection, meant to measure how faithful the engines are on real speech. It is off by default. Once enabled, audio files and text are kept on your disk only, under ~/Library/Application Support/Caspr/corpus. They stay under your control and are never uploaded. The recording bar states at all times that collection is on, and lets you stop it.

4. This site

This site uses no analytics cookie, no tracker and no remote font. It queries GitHub's public interface to display the latest published version number.

5. Contact

For any question: contact@lyriastudio.fr.
